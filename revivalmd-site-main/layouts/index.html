About Revival MD

Where Medical Expertise
Meets Artistry

Led by Dr. M. Miz. Haque, MD, MHA, Dr. Haroon Arshad, MD, MBA, and Sanna Arshad, PA-C, MHA, RevivalMD combines medical expertise with a genuine eye for aesthetics. Every treatment plan is built around your skin, your features, and what you actually want to change. Not someone else's ideal. Yours.

We're a physician-led aesthetic clinic with two med spa locations in New Jersey, one in Rahway (Union County) and one in Fair Lawn (Bergen County). So whether you're coming from Clark, Westfield, Scotch Plains, or anywhere nearby, you've got a board-certified team close to home.

Your first visit starts with a real conversation, not a sales pitch. What's bothering you? What have you tried before? What kind of results are you actually looking for? We'll figure out the right treatment together. No pressure, no upselling.

As a medical spa Rahway NJ families keep coming back to, we offer cosmetic injectables like Botox, advanced skin care treatments including facial rejuvenation and microneedling, laser hair removal (we're one of the few Splendor X providers in New Jersey), PRP, RF skin tightening, IV wellness therapy, exosome therapy, and medically supervised weight loss. But here's what matters most: every procedure is performed or directly supervised by our medical team. That's not a tagline. It's how we've always done it.
