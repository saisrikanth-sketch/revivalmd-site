Our Technology

Why the Lumenis Splendor X?

RevivalMD is one of the few practices in New Jersey to offer the Lumenis Splendor X — a next-generation laser platform that combines Alexandrite (755nm) and Nd:YAG (1064nm) wavelengths in a single handpiece. This dual-wavelength technology allows us to treat all skin types safely and effectively, from the lightest to the darkest complexions.

Dual-Wavelength Power

Alexandrite and Nd:YAG wavelengths blend or fire independently for precise, skin-type-optimized treatment.

Safe for All Skin Tones

Fitzpatrick skin types I through VI treated confidently — one of the few lasers truly effective on darker skin.

Faster Sessions

Large spot size and high repetition rate mean full legs can be completed in under 20 minutes.

Built-In Comfort

Integrated cooling system keeps the skin surface comfortable during treatment — no numbing cream needed.

The Splendor X is rare in New Jersey. Most clinics around here are still using older, single-wavelength systems. At RevivalMD, you're getting the latest platform with physician oversight on every session.

Splendor X full-body packages are priced on their own, separate from our standard laser sessions and membership pricing. During your consultation we'll walk you through every option, so you can pick what fits your goals and your budget.
