## What RF Microneedling Is and How It Works

RF microneedling is a procedure that uses tiny needles to deliver radiofrequency energy into the skin. The needles create controlled micro-injuries. At the same time, radiofrequency heat reaches deeper layers than traditional microneedling.

## RF Microneedling vs Traditional Microneedling

Both treatments help skin. They’re just not the same.

Traditional microneedling creates surface injury only. It improves texture and tone. RF microneedling adds controlled heat at depth, which leads to more noticeable tightening. Think of it as added power, used carefully.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many RF microneedling sessions do I need?
Most patients benefit from three sessions spaced four to six weeks apart. Your skin goals guide the plan. Some people add yearly maintenance.

### Is RF microneedling safe for darker skin?
Yes, when done by trained providers. RF energy targets deeper layers and lowers pigment risk. Physician oversight really matters here.

### Does RF microneedling hurt?
A numbing cream is used. Most people feel pressure and warmth, not pain. Discomfort is brief.

### Can RF microneedling help stretch marks?
It can improve stretch marks by boosting collagen and elasticity. Results vary based on age and depth of the marks.

### Who should avoid RF microneedling?
Pregnant patients, those with active infections, or certain implants may not be candidates. A medical consult is always required.
