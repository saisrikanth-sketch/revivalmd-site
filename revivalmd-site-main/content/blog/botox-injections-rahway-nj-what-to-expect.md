## What Is Botox and How Does It Work?

Botox Cosmetic is a purified form of botulinum toxin. It’s a neurotoxin injection that blocks nerve signals to targeted facial muscles. When those muscles relax, the skin above them smooths out.  

That simple action leads to softer lines.

This helps with dynamic wrinkles caused by movement, like forehead lines, crows feet, and severe frown lines. Botox doesn’t fill lines. It relaxes the muscles that cause them.  

That’s why dosing and placement matter so much for the patient.

Botox also supports medical care. Providers use this same injection for TMJ pain and excessive sweating. According to the American Academy of Dermatology, underarm sweating often improves for 4 to 6 months in many patients.

## What to Expect After Botox: Recovery and Results

After treatment, mild redness or bumps can show up. They often fade within an hour. Bruising can happen, but it’s uncommon for most patients.

![Botox Injections: What to Expect](/images/blog/botox-injections-rahway-nj-what-to-expect/botox-injections-rahway-nj-what-to-expect-inline-2.webp)

Patients usually see changes in 3 to 5 days. Full results appear in 10 to 14 days. Results often last about 3 to 4 months, though that can vary by patient.

Aftercare tips include:
- Don’t rub treated skin  
- Avoid workouts for 24 hours  
- Stay upright for a few hours  

These steps help protect results and support healthy skin.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does a Botox appointment take?
Most visits take about 30 minutes. The injection itself only takes a few minutes.

### Will Botox make me look frozen?
No. When done correctly, Botox keeps facial movement natural. The goal is balance for the patient.

### Can Botox replace plastic surgery?
Botox can delay or reduce the need for plastic surgery. It doesn’t replace surgery in every case.

### Is Botox safe?
Yes. When given by trained providers, neurotoxins have a strong safety record.

### Can I combine Botox with other treatments?
Yes. Many patients combine Botox with filler, [laser hair](/laser-hair-removal-rahway-nj-guide/) removal, or [skin rejuvenation](/exosome-therapy-nj-regenerative-aesthetics/) treatments.

### How often will I need repeat treatments?
Most patients return every 3 to 4 months. Timing depends on muscle strength, goals, and how your body responds.
