## What Laser Hair Removal Is and How It Works

[Laser hair removal](/laser-hair-removal-rahway-nj-guide/) is a medical procedure that uses focused light to target pigment in the hair follicle. This laser treatment sends heat into the follicle, which slows future hair growth without harming the surrounding skin. Over time, regrowth becomes finer and lighter.

Hair grows in cycles, and only hair in the active phase responds. That’s why a laser hair removal treatment is done as a series, not one visit. Each laser hair removal session is spaced weeks apart to match natural cycles.

Here’s the honest truth. This is reduction, not total removal. According to the American Academy of Dermatology, results are long term but not absolute. Many people reach smooth skin goals with maintenance, and that’s normal.
