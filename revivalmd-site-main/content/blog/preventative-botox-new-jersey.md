## What Is Preventative Botox?

Preventative Botox is a low-dose botulinum toxin treatment used before deep facial wrinkles form. It targets overactive facial muscles that crease the skin again and again. Over time, those movements break down collagen. Slowly, but surely.

![preventative botox NJ - What Is Preventative Botox?](/images/blog/preventative-botox-new-jersey/preventative-botox-new-jersey-inline-1.webp)

This type of botox injection focuses on areas like forehead lines, frown lines, and crow’s feet. These are common spots for dynamic wrinkles. Dynamic wrinkles show up with movement, not at rest.

Here’s the thing. Preventative botox is not the same as treating etched lines. The goal is to reduce strain, not eliminate motion. Patients still smile. They still frown. They just do it with less stress on the skin.

## Preventative Botox vs Traditional Botox for Established Wrinkles
