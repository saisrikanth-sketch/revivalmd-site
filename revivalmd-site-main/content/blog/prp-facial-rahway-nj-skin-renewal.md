## What Is a PRP Facial and How Does It Work?

A PRP facial is a skin treatment that uses platelet-rich plasma taken from your own blood. A small blood sample is drawn and placed in a centrifuge. This process separates platelets that contain growth factors used in healing. These signals help your skin repair itself over time.

PRP therapy works best when combined with microneedling. Tiny channels are created in the skin so PRP can absorb where it counts. This step also triggers collagen production on its own. Together, they support skin rejuvenation in a gradual, natural way.

Unlike a chemical peel or filler, PRP doesn’t add volume or strip layers away. It encourages your skin to act younger. Many people call it a [vampire facial](/tags/vampire-facial/), but the science matters more than the nickname. Results build slowly, which usually looks more natural.
