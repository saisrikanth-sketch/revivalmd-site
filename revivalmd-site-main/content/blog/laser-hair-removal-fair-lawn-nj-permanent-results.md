## What Is Laser Hair Removal

Laser hair removal is a laser treatment that uses focused light to target the hair follicle. The energy heats the follicle and limits future hair growth. Over time, treated strands become finer, lighter, or stop growing.

Laser hair removal treatment focuses on long-term reduction, not total removal. That matters. According to the American Academy of Dermatology, most patients see about 80 to 90 percent lasting reduction after a full series. This is why providers describe results as reduction, not perfection.

Laser hair removal works at the root of the hair follicle. That’s why results build slowly and why physician oversight matters for skin tone, skin type, and safety.

> Key Point: Long-term reduction means less regrowth, fewer ingrowns, and easier upkeep.

## How Laser Hair Removal Works and Why Multiple Sessions Matter

Hair grows in cycles. Lasers only work during the active growth phase. Not all hair grows at the same time. That’s the challenge.

Most plans include six to eight sessions spaced four to six weeks apart. This timing allows treatment of new growth as it appears. Laser hair removal fair lawn nj permanent results improve gradually, session by session.
