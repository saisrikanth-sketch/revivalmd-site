## What Is Microneedling and PRP?

Microneedling is a controlled treatment that uses tiny needles to create precise micro-injuries in the treatment area. These signals tell the skin to repair itself and start collagen induction therapy.

PRP stands for platelet rich plasma. It’s made from your own blood. After a simple draw, plasma rich in growth factors is prepared. These growth factors support repair and skin recovery.

When combined, microneedling and PRP work together. The micro channels help the plasma reach deeper layers. This is why a prp facial is sometimes called a vampire facial, though the real goal is medical skin health, not hype.

## How Microneedling with PRP Works

Here’s what happens during a typical treatment. First, your provider reviews your history and goals. The skin is cleaned and numbed for comfort.

Next, a microneedling device such as SkinPen microneedling or the Eclipse Micropen creates controlled micro channels using sterile tiny needles. This step activates repair right away.

Then PRP is applied. The plasma absorbs into the deeper layers through those channels. Over time, this supports collagen, skin elasticity, and healthier skin structure.

### Why PRP Makes a Difference

PRP therapy works because growth factors guide healing. They help fibroblasts build new collagen and support blood flow in the treated area.

Over time, skin looks smoother and stronger. Healing quality improves. This is why a prp treatment is also used for hair restoration, breast reconstruction, and breast augmentation recovery in medical care.

### Timeline and Recovery
