## GLP-1 / Semaglutide Therapy — The Science of Appetite Control

**GLP-1 receptor agonists** — including semaglutide (the active ingredient in Ozempic and Wegovy) and tirzepatide — represent a landmark advance in obesity medicine. These medications mimic GLP-1, a gut hormone released after eating, to communicate satiety signals to the brain, slow the rate at which food leaves the stomach, and improve the body's response to insulin.

The practical effect is a significant, sustained reduction in hunger and caloric intake without willpower struggle. Clinical trials published in the New England Journal of Medicine demonstrated an average body weight reduction of 15 to 20 percent over 68 weeks in patients using semaglutide with lifestyle intervention — results previously achievable only through bariatric surgery.

At RevivalMD, GLP-1 therapy is always physician-prescribed, carefully dosed, and monitored with regular lab work and follow-up visits. We do not operate a prescription-by-mail model — we provide real medical oversight because your health depends on it.
