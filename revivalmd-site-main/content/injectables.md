## Botox & Letybo — Smooth Expression Lines

**Botox and Letybo** are FDA-approved neuromodulators that temporarily relax the facial muscles responsible for dynamic wrinkles — the lines that form when you squint, smile, or furrow your brow. Treatment takes under 30 minutes, results appear within days, and most patients enjoy smooth, refreshed skin for three to four months per session.

Common treatment areas include forehead lines, frown lines between the brows (the "11s"), crow's feet, and the lip area. Botox is also used therapeutically for conditions like TMJ clenching, excessive sweating (hyperhidrosis), and chronic migraines.

## Dermal Fillers — Restore Volume and Contour

As we age, the face loses collagen, fat, and bone density — resulting in hollowed cheeks, deepening folds, thinning lips, and a less defined jawline. **Dermal fillers** use hyaluronic acid gel to instantly restore this lost volume, redefine facial contours, and smooth deep folds like nasolabial lines and marionette lines.

Results are immediate and natural-looking, lasting twelve to eighteen months depending on the product and placement. Our providers use a conservative, anatomy-first approach — the goal is always to look like a well-rested, refreshed version of yourself.

[Explore our dermal filler treatments](/dermal-fillers-rahway-nj/)

## PRP Injections — Regenerative Renewal

**Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy** takes regenerative medicine into the aesthetics space. We draw a small sample of your blood, process it to concentrate the growth factors, and inject the resulting plasma into areas of the face or scalp. These growth factors signal tissue repair, stimulate collagen synthesis, and — on the scalp — reactivate dormant hair follicles to restore density.

Because PRP uses your own biology, it is extremely well-tolerated with a very low risk of reaction. Results build gradually over six to twelve weeks as new collagen forms and follicles respond, producing improvements that look and feel completely natural.
