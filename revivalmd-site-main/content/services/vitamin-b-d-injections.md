what_is: |
  **Vitamin B and D injections** are intramuscular injections that deliver therapeutic doses of essential nutrients — including B12 (methylcobalamin), B-complex vitamins, and Vitamin D3 — directly into the muscle tissue, bypassing the gastrointestinal tract for near-complete absorption compared to the significantly lower bioavailability of oral supplements. These vitamins are critical for cellular energy production, neurological function, immune regulation, mood stability, and skin and hair health — deficiencies in either are common and frequently go undetected. At RevivalMD, Dr. Haroon Arshad and Sanna Arshad, PA-C administer these injections as part of a broader approach to optimizing patients' health and wellness from the inside out.
