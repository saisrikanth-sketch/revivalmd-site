  - question: "How many IPL sessions will I need?"

    answer: "Patients typically describe the sensation as a quick snap similar to a rubber band against the skin. It's brief and very tolerable. We can apply a topical numbing agent if you're concerned about comfort."

    answer: "Downtime is minimal. You may notice some redness and mild swelling for a few hours. Dark spots often darken initially before flaking off naturally over the following week. Most patients feel comfortable returning to work and social activities the same day or next day."

    answer: "IPL works best on lighter to medium skin tones. Darker skin types have a higher risk of pigmentation changes with traditional IPL. During your consultation, your provider will assess your skin and determine whether IPL is the safest and most effective option for you."
  - question: "What skin concerns does IPL treat?"
    answer: "IPL is effective for sun spots, age spots, freckles, redness from rosacea, broken capillaries, uneven skin tone, and mild acne scarring. It's one of the most versatile light-based treatments available."

    answer: "You'll begin to notice improvement within a week or two of your first treatment as darkened spots flake off and redness fades. Results continue to improve with each session, and the full effect of a complete series is typically visible about a month after your last treatment."

what_is: |
  **Intense Pulsed Light (IPL)** is a non-ablative, light-based skin treatment that emits broad-spectrum pulses of high-intensity light across multiple wavelengths, selectively targeting melanin in pigmented lesions and oxyhemoglobin in dilated blood vessels to break them down — without disrupting the skin's surface. This makes it highly effective for correcting sun damage, brown spots, redness from rosacea, and broken capillaries, while also triggering mild collagen remodeling to improve overall skin texture. At RevivalMD, IPL is used to help patients achieve a noticeably clearer, more even complexion without invasive procedures or extended downtime.
what_to_expect: |
  Immediately after IPL, your skin may look slightly pink or flushed, similar to a mild sunburn. This typically calms within a few hours. Dark spots will often appear darker for several days before they naturally crust and flake away. That darkening is actually a sign the treatment is working.
  
  Over the following week, you'll watch your skin gradually reveal a clearer, more even complexion underneath. Each subsequent session builds on the last, and most patients see significant improvement by the end of their treatment series.
  
  It's important to avoid direct sun exposure and wear SPF 30 or higher daily throughout your treatment course. Sun protection isn't just aftercare for IPL; it's what helps you maintain your results long term.
ideal_candidates: |
  IPL is well suited for adults with fair to medium skin tones who are dealing with sun damage, brown spots, redness, broken capillaries, or overall uneven tone. It's a popular choice for patients who want visible improvement without the recovery time of more aggressive resurfacing treatments.
  
  Good candidates are willing to commit to sun protection and a series of treatments for optimal results. Patients who are pregnant, have active skin infections, or use certain photosensitizing medications may need to wait or explore other options.
