what_is: |
  **PRP Biofiller** is a next-generation version of platelet-rich plasma that's been processed into a gel-like consistency so it works as a natural, autologous filler. In other words, it's made entirely from your own blood. We draw a small sample, spin it in a centrifuge to separate out the plasma components, then gently heat and cool the platelet-poor portion to form a soft, viscous gel. That gel is then recombined with concentrated PRP and injected into areas where you've lost volume.

  Here's how it's different from standard PRP. Regular PRP is a liquid that's injected to trigger collagen over several weeks. You don't really see immediate volume, it's more of a rejuvenation treatment. PRP Biofiller does both. The gel structure gives you visible volume the same day, and the trapped platelets keep releasing growth factors over the following months, so results tend to come on faster and last longer than liquid PRP alone.

  And it's different from hyaluronic acid fillers like Juvederm or Restylane, too. Those are lab-made gels that physically fill space. PRP Biofiller is built from your own biology, so there's essentially no risk of allergic reaction, granulomas, or the kind of migration issues some patients run into with synthetic products. It's a nice option if you want real volume but prefer to avoid anything foreign in your face.
