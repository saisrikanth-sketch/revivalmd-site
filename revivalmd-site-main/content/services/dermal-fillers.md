    answer: "Most patients describe mild discomfort rather than pain. We use topical numbing cream before treatment, and many fillers contain lidocaine for added comfort. The injections themselves take just minutes."

    answer: "Botox relaxes muscles that cause expression lines like forehead wrinkles and crow's feet. Fillers add volume beneath the skin to smooth deeper lines, restore lost fullness, and enhance contours. Many patients benefit from both."

    answer: "Downtime is minimal. You may have slight swelling, redness, or bruising at the injection sites for a day or two. Most patients return to their normal routine the same day."

what_is: |
  **Dermal fillers** are injectable gels — most commonly formulated from hyaluronic acid, a substance naturally present in the skin — that restore lost facial volume, smooth moderate-to-deep wrinkles, and sculpt contours without surgery or significant recovery time. They directly counteract the age-related loss of collagen, subcutaneous fat, and bone density that causes hollowing, sagging, and deepening lines across the face. At RevivalMD, Sanna Arshad, PA-C performs dermal filler treatments with an emphasis on anatomical precision and natural-looking results that enhance each patient's features without creating an overdone appearance.
what_to_expect: |
  Immediately after treatment, you'll notice improved volume and smoother contours. Some swelling and possible light bruising are normal and typically resolve within a few days. We recommend avoiding strenuous exercise, alcohol, and excessive heat for 24 hours.
  
  Filler results continue to refine over the first two weeks. Many patients come back and tell us they got compliments from friends and family who simply said they looked well-rested or healthy, which is exactly the kind of feedback we aim for.
  
  Maintenance sessions are usually needed every 6 to 18 months depending on the product used and your metabolism. Over time, consistent treatments can actually stimulate some of your own collagen production in the treated areas.
