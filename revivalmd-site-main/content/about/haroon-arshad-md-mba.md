## Dr. Haroon Arshad, MD, MBA

**Co-Founder & Medical Director, RevivalMD Aesthetic Clinic, NJ**

Dr. Haroon Arshad is a board-certified family medicine physician, entrepreneur, and one of the founders of RevivalMD Aesthetic Clinic in Rahway and Fair Lawn, New Jersey. He's spent over a decade in clinical practice across primary care, geriatric medicine, wound care, and urgent care, treating patients in more than 20 states. That breadth of experience shows up in how he runs RevivalMD. Every treatment plan starts with a real medical assessment, not a sales pitch. His dual MD/MBA background means patients get rigorous clinical judgment alongside a practice that's actually built around them.

### Credentials & Board Certification

- **MD**, St. Matthew's University School of Medicine (Class of 2011)
- **Residency**, Family Medicine, Hackensack Meridian Mountainside Medical Center, Glen Ridge, NJ (2015 to 2018)
- **MBA**, Master of Business Administration
- **Board Certified**, American Board of Family Medicine (ABFM)
- Licensed to practice medicine in 20 states

Board certification by the ABFM isn't a formality. It requires passing rigorous written exams and keeping up with continuing education every few years. For aesthetic patients, that matters more than people realize. Dr. Arshad can read your overall health picture before recommending anything, spot contraindications a non-physician injector might miss, and handle complications with the full resources of a trained clinician.

His MBA adds something else. RevivalMD is built as a cohesive, patient-first practice, not a franchise or a volume-driven spa. Every operational decision starts with the patient experience.

### Areas of Clinical Focus

- Aesthetic medicine: neuromodulators (Botox, Dysport), dermal fillers, and combination treatments
- Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy for skin rejuvenation and hair restoration
- Microneedling and collagen induction therapy
- Preventive and anti-aging medicine
- Primary care, geriatric medicine, and wound care

### Why Aesthetic Medicine

Dr. Arshad's interest in aesthetics grew out of a simple idea: feeling well and looking well are connected. After years in primary care, he saw how closely confidence and mental wellness track with how patients feel about their appearance. Done safely and thoughtfully, evidence-based aesthetic treatments make a real difference in quality of life. That's why he founded RevivalMD. To bring that level of medical rigor to aesthetic care in New Jersey.

### Philosophy of Care

> "My goal is to help every patient understand their options, then help them feel like the best version of themselves. Whether it's a preventive Botox appointment or something more comprehensive, the approach is always individualized."

Dr. Arshad believes aesthetic medicine works best when it's grounded in real medical understanding. He puts patient education above everything else. Every procedure gets explained. Expectations get set realistically. The goal is long-term treatment relationships, not one-off visits. And nobody at RevivalMD gets pressured into anything. Patients are guided toward what actually serves their goals.

### Continuing Education in Aesthetics

Dr. Arshad pursues ongoing training in advanced injectable techniques, facial anatomy, and emerging aesthetic modalities. That continuing education, layered on top of his family medicine foundation, keeps RevivalMD current with best practices and the most effective, evidence-supported treatments available.
