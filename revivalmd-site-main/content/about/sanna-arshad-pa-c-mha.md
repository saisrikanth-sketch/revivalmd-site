## Sanna Arshad, PA-C, MHA

**Physician Assistant & Aesthetic Specialist, RevivalMD Aesthetic Clinic, NJ**

Sanna Arshad is a board-certified physician assistant with over nine years of clinical experience across aesthetic medicine, adult and pediatric neurology, urgent care, and primary care. She's certified by the National Commission on Certification of Physician Assistants (NCCPA), the only national certifying body for PAs in the United States. What patients notice most is the combination: precise clinical technique paired with a warm, patient-first approach that puts even first-time injectable patients completely at ease. At RevivalMD, Sanna is the provider most patients see for Botox, fillers, and skin treatments. Her results reflect the care and artistry she brings to every appointment.

### Credentials

- **PA-C (Physician Assistant, Certified)**, Physician Assistant Studies, St. John's University, Queens, NY
- **MHA (Master of Healthcare Administration)**, Drexel University
- **Board Certified**, National Commission on Certification of Physician Assistants (NCCPA)

**What PA-C means for patients:** Sanna completed an accredited graduate-level PA program, passed national board exams, and maintains her certification through ongoing continuing medical education. Physician assistants in New Jersey are licensed to perform medical and aesthetic procedures under physician supervision. In Sanna's case, that's close collaboration with Dr. Haroon Arshad, MD, MBA.

**What MHA means for patients:** The Master of Healthcare Administration is a graduate degree focused on the business, ethics, policy, and quality systems behind excellent healthcare delivery. For RevivalMD patients, it means Sanna brings clinical skill plus a thoughtful understanding of what makes a healthcare experience genuinely good. From informed consent and clear communication to follow-up care and outcomes tracking.

### Aesthetic Specialties & Treatments

- Neuromodulators: Botox and Dysport for facial lines, preventive treatment, and jaw/masseter slimming
- Dermal fillers: lip augmentation, cheek contouring, under-eye hollows, and nasolabial folds
- Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy for facial rejuvenation and hair restoration
- Microneedling with and without PRP
- Laser hair removal and skin resurfacing treatments
- Comprehensive aesthetic consultations and personalized treatment planning
