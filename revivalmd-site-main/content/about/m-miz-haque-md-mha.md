## Dr. M. Miz. Haque, MD, MHA

**Founder & Physician, RevivalMD Aesthetic Clinic, NJ**

Dr. Muhammad Misbah-Ul Haque is a board-certified internal medicine physician with over 25 years of clinical experience. He's licensed in both New Jersey and New York, and brings deep medical knowledge and patient care expertise to RevivalMD. His background in internal medicine, combined with a Master of Health Administration, gives him a unique perspective on patient safety, clinical outcomes, and the operational excellence that defines how RevivalMD runs.

### Credentials & Board Certification

- **MD**, Ross University School of Medicine (Class of 2001, with honors)
- **Residency**, Internal Medicine, Interfaith Medical Center, Brooklyn, NY
- **MHA**, Master of Health Administration, George Washington University
- **Board Certified**, American Board of Internal Medicine (ABIM)
- Licensed in New Jersey and New York

Board certification by the American Board of Internal Medicine reflects a rigorous commitment to evidence-based medicine and ongoing professional development. For RevivalMD patients, Dr. Haque's internal medicine expertise adds an extra layer of safety. He can evaluate your overall health, flag potential contraindications, and make sure every aesthetic treatment is medically appropriate.

### Hospital Affiliations

- Bergen New Bridge Medical Center, Paramus, NJ
- The Valley Hospital, Ridgewood, NJ
- Mount Sinai West / Mount Sinai Morningside, New York, NY
- New York-Presbyterian Hospital, New York, NY

### Languages

Dr. Haque provides care in English, Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Spanish, and French. That reflects RevivalMD's commitment to serving the diverse communities of northern and central New Jersey.

### Education & Training

Dr. Haque graduated with honors from Ross University School of Medicine, then completed his internal medicine residency at Interfaith Medical Center in Brooklyn. He also trained at the Medical College of Georgia. His Master of Health Administration from George Washington University gives him both the clinical and administrative insight that shapes RevivalMD's physician-led model of care.
