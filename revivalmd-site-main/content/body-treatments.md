## Laser Hair Removal — Permanent Reduction, Any Body Area

**Laser hair removal** at RevivalMD uses advanced laser technology to target and destroy hair follicles at the root, preventing regrowth across virtually any area of the body — legs, arms, underarms, bikini, back, chest, face, and more. The laser selectively heats melanin in the follicle while leaving surrounding skin unharmed.

A typical series of six to eight sessions spaced four to eight weeks apart achieves up to 90% long-term reduction. Sessions are quick — legs can be treated in under an hour — and discomfort is minimal with our cooling technology. Say goodbye to the endless cycle of shaving, waxing, and ingrown hairs.

## RF Therapy — Non-Surgical Body Tightening

**RF (Radiofrequency) Therapy** delivers controlled energy waves deep into the skin's dermis and subdermal layers, generating heat that stimulates collagen production and triggers gradual tissue contraction. The result is firmer, smoother skin in areas affected by laxity — including the abdomen, upper arms, inner thighs, and knees.

RF Therapy is particularly popular for patients who have experienced skin laxity following weight loss or pregnancy, and who want meaningful improvement without surgery or extended recovery. Treatments are comfortable, require no anesthesia, and involve no downtime.

RevivalMD offers customized IV formulations targeting specific goals: energy and fatigue recovery, immune strengthening, athletic performance, skin luminosity, and hangover relief.

Sessions typically run 30 to 60 minutes in our comfortable infusion suite. Our medical team reviews your health intake before every session to ensure your formulation is safe and optimized. Many patients incorporate regular IV therapy into their monthly wellness routine for sustained benefits.

## Vitamin B12 & D3 Injections — Targeted Nutritional Support

**Vitamin B12 and D3 deficiencies** are extraordinarily common — affecting an estimated one in three adults in the US — and are linked to chronic fatigue, low mood, poor concentration, weakened immunity, and bone health decline. Oral supplements have limited absorption; injections deliver these nutrients directly into muscle tissue for immediate, complete bioavailability.

Our quick, in-office B12 and D3 injections take just minutes and are an excellent complement to IV therapy or as a standalone maintenance treatment between infusions. Many patients notice a meaningful boost in energy, clarity, and mood within days of their first injection.
