**Skin rejuvenation treatments** at RevivalMD in Rahway and Fair Lawn, NJ target the most common skin concerns — fine lines, acne scars, enlarged pores, sun damage, uneven tone, and loss of firmness — using medical-grade technology and evidence-based protocols. Our board-certified team customizes every treatment to your skin type, concerns, and goals.

From deep collagen remodeling to instant-glow facials, our menu of skin treatments offers a solution for every patient and every budget. Here is what each treatment does and who it is best suited for.

## Microneedling — Collagen Induction Therapy

**Microneedling** creates thousands of precise microchannels in the skin, triggering the body's natural healing cascade to produce new collagen and elastin. This makes it one of the most effective treatments for acne scars, enlarged pores, uneven texture, fine lines, and overall skin dullness.

Sessions take approximately 60 minutes and involve topical numbing for comfort. Skin appears pink for 24 to 48 hours afterward before revealing progressively smoother, firmer results over the following weeks. A series of three to six sessions delivers optimal outcomes.

[Learn more about Microneedling at RevivalMD](/microneedling-rahway-nj/)

## RF Microneedling — Tighten, Remodel, Firm

**RF Microneedling** elevates traditional microneedling by delivering radiofrequency energy through the needles directly into the dermis, generating precisely controlled heat that triggers deep collagen remodeling and tissue contraction. The result is superior skin tightening and wrinkle reduction compared to microneedling alone.

This treatment is especially effective for skin laxity on the face and neck, deep acne scars, and patients who want more aggressive anti-aging results. It is suitable for all skin tones, making it a versatile cornerstone of our skin tightening menu.

[Explore RF Microneedling](/rf-microneedling-rahway-nj/)

## IPL Photofacial — Erase Sun Damage and Redness

**Intense Pulsed Light (IPL)** therapy uses broad-spectrum light pulses to selectively target and destroy melanin deposits and dilated blood vessels without damaging surrounding skin. It is the gold-standard treatment for sunspots, age spots, rosacea, broken capillaries, and diffuse redness.

After treatment, pigmented spots temporarily darken and flake off within one week, revealing clearer, more uniform skin beneath. A series of three to five sessions delivers dramatic improvement in overall skin clarity and tone.

[Learn more about IPL Photofacials](/intense-pulsed-light-ipl/)

## Aquafirme Facials — The No-Downtime Glow Treatment

**Aquafirme** combines exfoliation, extraction, and infusion in one multi-step facial using patented vortex technology to simultaneously cleanse pores and flood the skin with customized serums. It is the ideal treatment for patients who want immediately visible results with zero downtime.

Aquafirme is suitable for all skin types and can be performed before a special event. Regular monthly sessions maintain clear pores, healthy hydration levels, and a consistent radiant glow.

[Discover Aquafirme Facials](/aquafirme-facials/)

## Exosome Therapy — Next-Generation Skin Renewal

**Exosomes** are nano-sized vesicles loaded with growth factors and cell-signaling molecules that instruct skin cells to repair and regenerate at an accelerated rate. Applied topically following microneedling or RF microneedling — or injected — exosomes dramatically enhance healing speed, reduce post-treatment inflammation, and amplify the collagen-stimulating effects of any energy-based procedure.

Exosome therapy represents the cutting edge of regenerative aesthetics, and RevivalMD is one of the few NJ med spas offering this advanced treatment protocol.
