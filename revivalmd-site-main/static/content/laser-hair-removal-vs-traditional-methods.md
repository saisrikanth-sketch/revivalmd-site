# Laser Hair Removal vs. Traditional Hair Removal


Laser hair removal delivers 80–90% permanent hair reduction after a series of 6–8 sessions, making it more cost-effective and time-efficient than a lifetime of shaving or waxing. If you've ever been frustrated by the endless cycle of shaving, waxing, or plucking unwanted hair, you're not alone — most patients tell us they wish they'd made the switch sooner.

**Key Takeaways**
- Shaving only cuts hair at the surface, requiring daily or near-daily maintenance, while laser targets and destroys the follicle itself.
- Waxing lasts 3–4 weeks but requires hair to grow out between appointments and causes repeated skin irritation.
- Laser hair removal typically requires 6–8 sessions to achieve 80–90% permanent hair reduction.
- Over a lifetime, the cumulative cost of shaving supplies and waxing appointments often exceeds the upfront investment in laser treatment.
- Laser is safe for most skin types and body areas, with minimal downtime between sessions.

## Shaving

**Pros:** Quick, cheap, painless
**Cons:** Results last 1-3 days, razor burn, ingrown hairs, constant maintenance

Shaving cuts hair at the surface level, so it grows back almost immediately. Over a lifetime, the time spent shaving adds up to hundreds of hours.

## Waxing

**Pros:** Lasts 3-4 weeks, hair grows back finer over time
**Cons:** Painful, can cause skin irritation, ingrown hairs, requires hair to grow out between sessions, ongoing cost

Waxing removes hair from the root, which is more effective than shaving but comes with its own set of drawbacks — particularly discomfort and the inconvenience of needing visible hair growth between appointments.

## Depilatory Creams

**Pros:** Painless, easy to use at home
**Cons:** Chemical smell, can cause skin reactions, results last only a week, not suitable for all skin types

These products dissolve hair chemically. They work, but the results are short-lived and some patients experience sensitivity or allergic reactions.

## Laser Hair Removal

**Pros:** Long-lasting to permanent results, precision targeting, no ingrown hairs, smooth skin year-round
**Cons:** Requires multiple sessions, higher upfront cost, slight discomfort during treatment

Our [laser hair removal treatments](/laser-hair-removal-rahway-nj/) use concentrated light energy to target and destroy hair follicles. After a series of treatments (typically 6-8 sessions), most patients see 80-90% permanent hair reduction.
