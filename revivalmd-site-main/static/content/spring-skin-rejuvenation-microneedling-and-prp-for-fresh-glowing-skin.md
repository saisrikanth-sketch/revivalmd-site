# Spring Skin Rejuvenation: Microneedling and PRP for Fresh, Glowing Skin


Combining microneedling with PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy is one of the most effective ways to restore radiance and firmness after winter — the two treatments amplify each other's collagen-stimulating effects for accelerated, longer-lasting results. Winter can be tough on your skin, and spring is the perfect time to invest in treatments that help your skin bounce back.

**Key Takeaways**
- Microneedling triggers the skin's natural wound-healing response, stimulating collagen and elastin production to improve texture, reduce pores, and fade scars.
- PRP is derived from your own blood — the platelet-rich plasma contains growth factors that accelerate tissue repair and intensify the microneedling results.
- A combined microneedling + PRP session takes approximately 60–90 minutes from blood draw to aftercare guidance.
- Expect 24–48 hours of mild redness and swelling; most patients return to normal activities within one to two days.
- Spring's lower UV intensity makes it a safer window for treatment recovery compared to summer months.

## What Microneedling Does

[Microneedling](/microneedling-rahway-nj/) creates controlled micro-injuries in the skin's surface using tiny, sterile needles. Your body responds by ramping up collagen and elastin production — the two proteins responsible for keeping skin firm, smooth, and youthful.

The result? Improved texture, reduced scarring, smaller pores, and a noticeable glow that develops over the weeks following treatment.

## Why Add PRP?

[PRP therapy](/prp-facial-rahway-nj/) takes your own blood, concentrates the growth-factor-rich platelets, and applies them directly to your skin during the microneedling process. Think of it as supercharging your body's natural healing response.

The growth factors in PRP accelerate tissue repair, boost collagen production even further, and can help with issues like hyperpigmentation and under-eye concerns.

## The Combined Treatment Process

Here's what a typical session looks like at RevivalMD:

1. **Blood draw** — A small amount of blood is drawn and processed in a centrifuge to isolate the PRP
2. **Numbing** — A topical anesthetic is applied to keep you comfortable
3. **Microneedling** — The treatment is performed across the target areas
4. **PRP application** — Your concentrated PRP is applied directly onto the treated skin
5. **Recovery guidance** — We'll walk you through aftercare to maximize your results

The entire process takes about 60-90 minutes.

## What to Expect After Treatment

Some redness and mild swelling are normal for the first 24-48 hours — similar to a mild sunburn. Most patients feel comfortable returning to normal activities within a day or two.

You'll start noticing improvements within a week or two, with continued improvement over the following months as new collagen develops.

## Why Spring Is Ideal

After months of winter damage, your skin is ready for renewal. Spring's milder UV levels also mean reduced sun sensitivity risk during recovery, making it safer than scheduling during summer months.
