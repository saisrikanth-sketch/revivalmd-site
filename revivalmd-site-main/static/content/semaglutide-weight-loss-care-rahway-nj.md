## What Is Semaglutide and How Does It Work?

Semaglutide is a GLP 1 medication first prescribed for type 2 diabetes. Doctors noticed something else during clinical trials. Many patients also lost weight. Over time, that led to its use as a prescription medication for weight management under supervision.

Semaglutide works as a GLP 1 receptor agonist. It mimics a natural hormone released after eating. This signal lowers hunger, reduces food intake, and helps the body manage insulin release and glucagon secretion. It also interacts with the GIP receptor, which plays a role in energy balance.

The active ingredient slows digestion, so meals feel more filling. That often helps patients eat less without feeling deprived. Novo Nordisk developed semaglutide after years of research into blood sugar control and metabolic disease.

So no, it’s not magic. But it is science. And for many patients, it supports lasting change.
